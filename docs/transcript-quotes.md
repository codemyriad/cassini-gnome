# Turn quotation marks (D38.5)

When a turn is displayed, straight double quotes pair in reading order and become “ and ”. A final quote without a partner in that turn disappears, and an item left with only spaces disappears with it. Pairing never crosses a speaker turn: it runs over each consecutive run of one speaker, even where the reader and Library previews rejoin a turn around another speaker's overlapping interjection. Existing curly quotes and apostrophes stay as written.

Library previews, the Meeting transcript, the standalone transcript reader (including its in-window search), Search excerpts and Markdown export use this display projection. The timed items and original transcript files are unchanged. Even when a quote-only item disappears, transcript links and Search seek positions still address the original item.

The Meeting transcript first rejoins interrupted speaker runs using the same model as Library previews and the standalone reader, then splits long turns into paragraphs of at most 100 host items or 30 seconds. Brief overlapping replies appear inline at their original fragment seam, with a speaker label and word links. Sustained competing speech and clean exchanges stay separate. Original word indices connect folded replies to speaker listening; source gaps still prevent rejoining. Quotes still pair across the whole turn, so a quote opened in one paragraph closes in the next, as it does in Search and export.

The Library pull quote (R8) is not turn text: it stays verbatim, straight quotes included (LIBRARY-RULES R8.3, shared vector `r8-consecutive-quote`).

The formatting tests cover embedded pairs, pairs across timed items and across paragraph bounds, an orphan at a speaker boundary, quote-only items in the reader layout and search, and a quote-only item before a Search match. The Markdown goldens remain unchanged for their existing inputs.

The English synthetic transcript bodies in `tests/ux/interjections/{acknowledgment,competing}.words.json`, rendered by `tests/ui/interjections.py`, cover a brief “Sounds good.” inside Ada’s sentence (three old rows become one paragraph with an inline reply) and simultaneous sentences interleaved by word (22 old rows become two readable paragraphs). They contain no personal meeting data; the underlying test audio is tone/silence. The committed images in `docs/screenshots/interjections/` were captured from the real GTK Meeting page at the same size and theme: `before-*` uses unmodified baseline `c021e70`, and `after-*` uses this change.
